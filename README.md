# Bike Buyers Sales Analysis Dashboard

## Project Overview

Analysis of a 1,000-customer dataset to identify factors influencing bike purchase decisions. Built an interactive Excel dashboard using Pivot Tables, Pivot Charts, KPI cards, and Slicers to surface business insights across customer demographics and behaviour.

## Dataset

- **Records:** 1,000 customers
- **Features:** Gender, Marital Status, Age, Income, Education, Occupation, Home Owner, Cars, Region, Commute Distance, Purchased Bike

## Key Business Insights

- Single customers purchased more bikes than married customers.
- Middle-aged customers recorded the highest bike purchase count across all age groups.
- Medium-income customers purchased the most bikes despite not being the highest earners.
- Graduate degree holders had the highest bike purchase count by education level.
- Professionals purchased more bikes than any other occupation group.
- Customers with a 0-1 mile commute distance had the highest purchase rate — suggesting bikes are preferred for short commutes.
- North America showed the strongest bike purchase trend across all regions.
- Male customers purchased more bikes than female customers overall.
